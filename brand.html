AIBAST brand and style guide

The reference for every AIBAST Agents Library page, course and video. It keeps the look the site already has, Microsoft's Segoe type and the AIBAST raspberry accent, and adds quiet pictures that sit behind the words and help a reader know where they are.

3. What "subtle" means here

1A still life of one plain object from the work: a pallet, a permit stamp, a street cabinet, a folder of claims. Never a person, never a screen of made-up software.
2Soft paper ground, fine ink line and two grey tones. One small raspberry detail at most.
3At least 60% empty space, so text can sit over or beside it and stay easy to read.
4Calm light, no glow, no gradients, no 3D gloss, no robots, no brains, no sparkles.
5No words, logos, brand marks, customer names or faces of real people.
6Every picture in a set is drawn the same way, so 72 courses look like one shelf, not 72 posters.

5. Yes and no

Yes

A single building-permit stamp on paper, drawn in fine grey line, with a thin raspberry ink mark, set to the right with open space on the left.

No

A glowing blue brain, a robot at a laptop, a crowd of smiling people, a dashboard full of fake numbers, or any picture with words in it.

6. The picture recipe

Every picture is made from one fixed recipe, with only the object changed:

Quiet editorial still life of {one object from the course's work}, fine ink line drawing with two soft grey tones on warm off-white paper, one small raspberry (#b11f4b) accent detail, object placed right of centre, at least 60% empty space, calm even light, no text, no logos, no people, no screens, no gradients.

The object for each course is picked from that course's own description (for example, Account Intelligence: a closed leather folder of account notes).

7. Using this guide

1Use these colours and type on every new page. The same values are in brand.json for tools and builds.
2Make every new picture from the recipe above, changing only the object. Check it against the six "subtle" rules before it goes on a page.
3The AIBAST Academy is this guide applied: its pages, pictures and videos follow every rule here exactly, so when in doubt, copy what the academy does.
4New pictures and changes to this guide arrive through a pull request, so a person reviews them before they reach the site.
