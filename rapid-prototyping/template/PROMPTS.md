# What to say to your AI

Open this folder in the AI you use (GitHub Copilot, Claude, Cursor, Codex, Gemini, any of them) and
paste one of these. Fill in the blanks. That's all you need to know.

**Start**
> I want to prototype ____ . It should let someone ____ . Follow AGENTS.md, build the first version, start it, and show me.

**Add something**
> Add a way to ____ . Keep everything else working, run the check, and tell me what to try.

**Something looks wrong**
> When I ____ , I see ____ instead of ____ . Fix it, run the check, and show me.

**Make it safe to demo**
> I'm showing this to people. Make sure every screen still works if the AI is off (recorded answers), run the check, and give me a 2-minute walkthrough.

**Explain it to my team**
> Write one page, in plain words, about what this prototype does and what it would take to make it real.
