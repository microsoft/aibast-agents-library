Discovery call (synthetic sample) - facilities team at a regional manufacturer

Consultant: Walk me through how equipment requests work today.
Ops lead: Anyone on the floor fills in a form: what they need, which line, how urgent, rough cost. It lives in a spreadsheet on a shared drive and half the time someone has it locked.
Consultant: Then what?
Ops lead: I check it every morning. Under 500 dollars I just approve. Over 500 goes to the plant manager by email and we wait, sometimes a week.
Consultant: And after approval?
Ops lead: Purchasing gets an email, orders it, and marks it Ordered, then Received when it arrives. The requester never knows where it is, so they call me.
Consultant: What would good look like?
Ops lead: A form on their phone, approvals routed automatically, and the requester told when it's ordered and when it arrives. And a list of everything open, by line.
Plant manager: And I need to see what I'm approving on my phone without opening a spreadsheet.
