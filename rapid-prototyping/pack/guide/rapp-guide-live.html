For Solution Engineers

From a discovery call to a working prototype, on your laptop, before the next meeting.

This page walks you through the RAPP process live. Each step runs on your own Brainstem: your transcript, your prototype and your checks never leave your computer. You bring the call; your Brainstem does the heavy lifting; you make the calls at each gate.

Connect this page to your Brainstem

This page connects to your Brainstem when your Brainstem opens it. In your Brainstem chat, say "open the RAPP live guide". If it still says not connected, enter the code from the guide's log.

✓ Connected.

1

The discovery call

RAPP step 1. Paste your call transcript, or use the sample: a regional insurer that wants first notice of loss (FNOL) triaged faster. It's synthetic: no real people.

Templates come only from the AIBAST library's published registry, and each file's fingerprint is checked before it runs.

2

Transcript analysis Gate 1

Your Brainstem reads the call and writes DISCOVERY.md: the problem, who's involved (roles, never names), the data needed, how success is measured, the first slice to build, and what to ask the customer. It also creates the prototype folder and starts it.

3

The MVP "poke"

RAPP step 3. Hand the first slice to the AI you already use (GitHub Copilot, Claude, Cursor, Codex or Gemini). Open the prototype folder in it and paste this. It follows the folder's AGENTS.md: small steps, made-up data, and it stops at your gates.

4


Rehearse, then show the customer Gate 2


RAPP step 4. Ask your prototype what the customer would ask. Then show it to them on your laptop, and put what you heard back into DISCOVERY.md.









5–8


Build it out, keep it demo-safe Gate 3


RAPP steps 5 to 8. Keep talking to your AI in small loops; it runs the check before it tells you anything. Before anyone else sees it, say: "I'm showing this to people. Make sure every screen still works if the AI is off, run the check, and give me a 2-minute walkthrough." Then run the check here once more.

What this page can and can't do on your computer


    

  • This page is served by your own computer (127.0.0.1), not by a website. The small server behind it answers only this page, after pairing with a one-time code. No website can reach it.
    • 

  • The bridge offers a short fixed menu: create a prototype from the transcript you paste, check it, and chat with that prototype. No file access, no commands, no access to the Brainstem you use every day.
    • 

  • Your transcript goes from this page to your own computer only. It's kept inside the prototype's discovery/ folder, which is excluded from source control.
    • 

  • The bridge closes itself after 45 minutes idle. Your Brainstem is never changed.
    • 



Want something it doesn't do? Run brainstem-proto feedback "your idea".
