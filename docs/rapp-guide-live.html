Rapid prototyping, on your own Brainstem

From a discovery call to a working prototype, before the next meeting.

A guided walk through the RAPP process that runs entirely on your laptop. Your Brainstem does the heavy lifting; you decide at every gate. Nothing on this page connects to your computer, and nothing runs until you load it.

Load the agent: this is your sign-off

Everything the guide uses lives in one readable file. Downloading it and putting it in your Brainstem is your decision to run it; until then, nothing happens on your computer.

Download rapid_prototyping_agent.py

One file, about 110 KB, plain Python and text you can read before you load it. Fingerprint (sha256):

80a49cedf9876f57ecdb070ea36e785fcecb01e6c1efadefa4cf803310870d5e
  1. Have the Brainstem. If you don't yet, use the one-liner on the installer page.
  2. Check the file (recommended): 
    shasum -a 256 ~/Downloads/rapid_prototyping_agent.py
    It should print the fingerprint above. On Windows: Get-FileHash $HOME\Downloads\rapid_prototyping_agent.py
  3. Load it into your Brainstem by moving it into your Brainstem's agents folder: 
    mv ~/Downloads/rapid_prototyping_agent.py ~/.brainstem/src/rapp_brainstem/agents/
    Windows: Move-Item $HOME\Downloads\rapid_prototyping_agent.py $HOME\.brainstem\src\rapp_brainstem\agents\. Your Brainstem picks it up on your next message; no restart.
  4. In your Brainstem chat, say set up rapid prototyping once. It writes the guide's files beside your Brainstem, never inside it, and downloads nothing.
  5. Then say open the RAPP live guide. The guide opens in your browser, served by your own computer.

What the guide walks you through

RAPP stepOn your laptop
1. Discovery callPaste the call transcript, or use the built-in sample (a regional insurer triaging claims).
2. Transcript analysis Gate 1Your Brainstem writes DISCOVERY.md: the problem, who's involved (roles, never names), the data needed, success measures, the first slice to build, and what to ask the customer. It also creates the prototype, optionally starting from an AIBAST agent template such as Claims Processing. You confirm before anything else is built.
3. MVP "poke"Hand the first slice to the AI you already use (GitHub Copilot, Claude, Cursor and others) with one copied prompt. It builds in small steps on made-up data and runs a plain-language check before it tells you anything.
4. Customer validation Gate 2Rehearse by asking your prototype the customer's questions, then show it on your laptop.
5–8. Build and demo review Gate 3Keep building in small loops; make it demo-safe so every screen works even if the AI is off.
9–14. ProductionContinue with the production guide. The same agent files move to the Azure Function Brainstem.

Data

Use the sample, synthetic data, or content you are approved to process with your Brainstem's AI. Customer content, including discovery-call transcripts, needs your data-handling approval first. The guide asks you to confirm this before it uses anything other than the sample.

What it does on your computer

  • Writes the guide's files to ~/.brainstem-proto and adds a brainstem-proto command. Your Brainstem itself is never changed.
  • Serves the guide from your own computer only. The small server behind it answers only that page, after a one-time pairing, and closes itself after 45 idle minutes. No website can reach it.
  • Uses the network only if you pick an AIBAST template, which is fetched from this library and checked against its published fingerprint before use.
  • Stores no secrets and sends nothing anywhere. To remove it, say uninstall rapid prototyping (your prototypes are kept). To turn it off on a computer, set RAPID_PROTOTYPING_DISABLED=1.

Something missing? In your prototype folder, run brainstem-proto feedback "your idea".
