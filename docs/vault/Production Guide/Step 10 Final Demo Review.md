# Step 10 Final Demo Review

**Final Demo Review** is **Quality Gate #5**.
The customer confirms the polished video accurately represents their solution.
Once approved, the demo is locked and can be shared with executives, board members, or field teams.

Guide section: [Step 10 in the production guide](../rapp-guide.html#step10)

## What this step is

- **Goal:** final customer approval of the video demo.
- **Time:** 30-60 minutes (one review call).
- **Decision:** approve for production use, or request adjustments.

## Who does it

- **Delivery team:** walks the customer through the demo and the checklist.
- **Customer:** checks facts, tone, and branding, and grants distribution permission.

## Inputs (from Step 09)

- **Video demo** (HTML or MP4) with Azure TTS narration, from [[Step 09 Generate Video Demo]].
- **Demo JSON:** the full script with steps, metrics, and narration.
- **Distribution plan:** email, hosted URL, or embedded in a deck.

## Review checklist

| Review item | What to check |
|---|---|
| **Metrics accuracy** | All numbers match reality: time saved, ROI, accuracy gains. |
| **Messaging tone** | Narration fits the audience (executives vs. field users). |
| **Company branding** | Customer name spelled correctly; no competitor references. |
| **Technical accuracy** | Integration points and systems mentioned are correct. |
| **Shareability** | Customer is comfortable with internal distribution. |

## The gate: two outcomes

- **APPROVED.** The demo is locked. Proceed to [[Step 11 Production Planning]].
- **ITERATE.** Minor fixes to narration, metrics, or branding. Fix and re-review.

## Outputs

- **Approved demo files:** final HTML or MP4, locked for distribution.
- **Distribution permission:** customer sign-off for executive sharing.
- **Usage guidelines:** who can see it and how to present it.

## Where this sits in the timeline

The guide groups Steps 9-10 as about **one day** of work.
After this gate, the project leaves the prototype phase.
Steps 11-14 are the production phase, mostly owned by the customer's IT team.

## How rapp/1 underpins this step

This is a human review gate. The upstream RAPP/1 specification does not define it.
The practical tie-in: "metrics accuracy" and "technical accuracy" are only checkable because the demo shows the real deployed agents, not a mockup. Those agents follow [[The Agent Contract]].
