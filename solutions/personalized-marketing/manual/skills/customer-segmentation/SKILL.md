# Privacy-safe customer segmentation

Use for portfolio questions about customer groups, engagement, or contribution.

1. Read the aggregate segment table from the synthetic-records knowledge file.
2. Compare size, orders, spend, categories, channels, and engagement.
3. Exclude demographic and sensitive attributes.
4. Explain the result in Marketing Director language: priorities, governance,
   and qualitative business value.

Output a compact table and review priorities. State that no individual was
profiled and no campaign, message, offer, reward, or purchase action occurred.
