Production replacement seams

  • Replace manual knowledge files with approved, governed customer sources while preserving grounding and citation boundaries.
  • Replace recommendation-only skill seams with approved tools only after identity, authorization, confirmation, and success evidence are defined.
  • Keep publication, sharing, telemetry, retention, and support ownership as explicit production decisions.

The pilot must never claim a live lookup or external write unless an approved production tool returns evidence that it succeeded.

Evidence gates

  • Source: deployment source and isolated transcripts exist.
  • Local: every locked business-agent case passes.
  • Preview: every front-door case passes in a fresh chat.
  • Visual: only positively provable annotated checkpoints are displayed.
  • Draft: the package records published: false.
  • Customer: governance, telemetry, support, and success measures are agreed before production.
