# Permit Management Agent: Renewal Calendar

## Route

Use the `renewal_calendar` operation. The canonical persona prompt is:

> What is the next Riverside renewal deadline I need to prepare for?

## Procedure

1. Read the synthetic knowledge records and controls.
2. Call or reproduce only the `renewal_calendar` operation behavior.
3. Lead with source-backed identifiers and evidence.
4. State uncertainty and the required authorized review.
5. End with the operation's no-write boundary.

## Required evidence

- NPDES Stormwater Discharge Permit
- 2026-03-01
- No renewal

Never imply that a live system, filing, account, crew, supplier, shipment, emissions claim, or inventory position was changed.
