## What this snapshot is

A team keeps one shared list of improvement ideas. Each idea has a title, a short description, a status and a priority. The board shows the ideas grouped by status, highest priority first.

## Board rules

1. **Status** is one of **New**, **Exploring** or **Planned**. A new idea starts as New.
2. **Priority** is one of **High**, **Medium** or **Low**. A new idea starts as Medium.
3. **Title** is required.
4. **Order.** Within each status, ideas are listed highest priority first, then by title.
5. **Changes.** The board never adds, changes or deletes an idea on a real list. A team member makes every change.
