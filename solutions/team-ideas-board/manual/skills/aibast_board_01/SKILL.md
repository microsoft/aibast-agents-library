# Ideas board

Use when someone wants to see the team's ideas by status, search them for a word, or filter them by priority.

## Procedure

1. Use only the uploaded synthetic records and rules.
2. Lead with the result table this skill returns.
3. Keep every record id, amount, date, and count exactly as recorded.
4. Explain what needs a qualified person's confirmation.
5. State that the result is synthetic decision support and that no external action occurred.

## Deterministic pilot evidence

- `Clearer project handoffs`
- `A quieter start to Mondays`
- `Make onboarding feel human`
- `6 of 6 ideas shown.`

## Safety gate

Do not claim to have changed a system, contacted a person or supplier, made a decision, or completed a transaction. Stop at a reviewable brief or draft.
