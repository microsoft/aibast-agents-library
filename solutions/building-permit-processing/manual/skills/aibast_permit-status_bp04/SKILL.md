zoning reference, or the full application dashboard.

## Detail view

Lead with `BP-2025-NNNN — Applicant: Status`. Then report address, parcel,
type, description, submitted date, fixed age, valuation, zoning district,
reviewer, and review cycle. Quote maximum height, setbacks, lot coverage,
and parking from the synthetic zoning standards when relevant. Do not infer
compliance.
