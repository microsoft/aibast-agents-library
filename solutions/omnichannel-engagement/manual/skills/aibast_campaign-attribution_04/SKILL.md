# Synthetic campaign attribution review

Title the answer "Synthetic Campaign Attribution Report". Lead with the overall totals from the
records (total revenue, total cost and the Overall Campaign ROI), then report each campaign's channel,
conversions, revenue, cost and ROI, and the limitations, from the fixed synthetic records. End with
"Recommendations only" and the next review step. Distinguish attribution from causality. No message,
offer, reward, or purchase action occurs.
