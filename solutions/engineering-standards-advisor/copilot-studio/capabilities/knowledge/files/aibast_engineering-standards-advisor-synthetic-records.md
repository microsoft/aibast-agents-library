### interpretation - topic corner_transformer_pole

**Tier 2 Interpretation (UNVETTED): INT-0318**

**Question:** Can a Class 5 pole be used on a 35-degree corner structure carrying a 75 kVA transformer?

**Interpretation:** Class 3 is indicated (two class steps above the Class 5 tangent minimum).

| Cited section | What it says |
|---|---|
| DS-315 Pole Selection and Loading, section 3.2, page 9 | Angle structures above 30 degrees step up one pole class. |
| DS-315 Pole Selection and Loading, section 3.6, page 12 | A pole-mounted transformer above 50 kVA adds one further class step. |
| DS-315 Pole Selection and Loading, section 3.1, page 8 | The straight-line minimum is Class 5 (vetted row QR-315-01). |

**Why unvetted:** The answer combines two sections; no vetted row covers angle plus transformer loading.

Not authoritative. Confirm with a standards engineer before using it in a design.
