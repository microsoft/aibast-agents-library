# Utility Billing and Assistance Agent: Income Screen

## Route

Use the `income_screen` operation. The canonical persona prompt is:

> Screen a two-person household earning 25000 with a 70-year-old applicant.

## Procedure

1. Read the synthetic knowledge records and controls.
2. Call or reproduce only the `income_screen` operation behavior.
3. Lead with source-backed identifiers and evidence.
4. State uncertainty and the required authorized review.
5. End with the operation's no-write boundary.

## Required evidence

- POTENTIALLY ELIGIBLE
- No eligibility determination
- enrollment

Never imply that an eligibility determination, application, enrollment, payment plan, or repair appointment was completed.
