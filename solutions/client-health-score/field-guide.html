Client Health Score Agent

Use this guide to understand the workshop boundary, expected proof, production seams, and recovery paths before or during the hands-on module.

Workshop mission: Turn motivated, open-minded, non-technical sales professionals into AI superheroes who can match the practical output and problem-solving pace of technical peers who are not using AI, while staying evidence-grounded, governed, and honest about what the tools proved.
Evidence boundary: all packaged records and outcomes are synthetic qualitative evidence—not customer KPIs, measured production results, live connections, or publication approval.

Production replacement seams

  • Replace packaged synthetic inputs with an approved Dynamics 365 connection; preserve the reviewed input and output contract.
  • Replace packaged synthetic inputs with an approved Microsoft Teams connection; preserve the reviewed input and output contract.
  • Replace packaged synthetic inputs with an approved Outlook connection; preserve the reviewed input and output contract.

The pilot must never claim a live lookup or external write unless an approved production tool returns evidence that it succeeded.

Evidence gates

  • Source: deployment source and isolated transcripts exist.
  • Local: every locked business-agent case passes.
  • Preview: every front-door case passes in a fresh chat.
  • Visual: only approved annotated checkpoints count as proof; review-required captures may appear only as clearly labeled orientation references.
  • Draft: the package records published: false.
  • Customer: governance, telemetry, support, and success measures are agreed before production.

Failure recovery

SymptomRecovery

A case misses a markerKeep the case failed and inspect the package. Never retry until it happens to pass.
